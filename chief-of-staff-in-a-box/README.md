# Chief of Staff in a Box

Build your own AI chief of staff with Claude. It interviews you about how you actually work, lets you name your chief of staff, your command center and your brand, then builds the whole system: a brain dump that sorts itself, a command center, daily and weekly routines, a list of the work you shouldn't be doing, goals and a weekly scorecard, a mistakes log, and (if you lead a team) a management team dashboard and 1:1 pages.

## Two ways to use it

| Format | Best for | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The skill folder** (`SKILL.md` and `modules/`) | Claude users who can add skills | Zip this folder, upload it as a skill, then say "build me a chief of staff." |
| **PROMPT.md** | Anyone | Paste the prompt into a new Claude chat or Claude Project and answer the questions. |

## What you get

* **Names you choose** for your chief of staff, command center and brand
* **Operating manual** written in your words, read first every session
* **Brain dump and capture** with labels (Idea, Read, Decision, Win, Task, Waiting) and a weekly dump that sorts everything
* **Command center** with a Today view, three task views (mine, assigned out, waiting on), ideas, decisions, goals
* **Routines**: daily brief, midday check, weekly review, monthly cleanup, quarter review. Few on purpose.
* **Agents register**: every scheduled routine in one list, with what it reads, what it writes and when it last ran
* **Work you shouldn't be doing**: a delegation filter, a Friday friction scan and a stop doing list
* **Goals and rocks**: yearly theme, quarterly rocks, weekly scorecard
* **How you work**: work style profile, mistakes log (every correction becomes a rule), voice learnings, 30 day noise audit, pressure test
* **Management team dashboard** (optional): Tuesday style meeting page, scorecard, rocks, issues, wins, department roundup
* **1:1 pages** (optional): one per direct report, with commitments both ways
* **Chief of staff to chief of staff messages** (optional): for leaders who each get their own
* **Live pages** (if your Claude account can publish pages): your command center, team dashboard and 1:1 pages as private pages that open in a browser, shared only with the people you name. If your account can't, it falls back to files, a doc or a spreadsheet.
* **Safe defaults**: nothing is sent without your yes, nothing is invented, private things stay private

## Quick start

1. Add the skill (or paste `PROMPT.md`).
2. Say: "Build me a chief of staff."
3. Answer the questions in small groups. Messy answers and voice memo transcripts are perfect.
4. Read what it builds. Fix anything that doesn't sound like you.
5. Run the daily brief once. Tell it what was noisy.

## Before you rely on it

This builds files and routines. It does not connect to your accounts by itself, and the result is only as good as your answers. Read every rule it writes, especially around sending messages, sharing pages, and handling private information. Start with read only connections and let the routines run cleanly for two weeks before you let anything write.

Your account may not support every format (scheduled tasks, published pages, connectors). The skill falls back to a prompt you paste by hand when something isn't available.
