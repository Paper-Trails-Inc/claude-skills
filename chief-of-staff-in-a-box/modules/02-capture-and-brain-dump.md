# Module 2: Capture and brain dump

The point: getting a thought out of {{MY_NAME}}'s head must take under ten seconds, and nothing captured is ever lost.

## One easy way in

Pick one primary door with the person and make it frictionless: a note to the chat, a "Send to {{CHIEF_NAME}}" shortcut on the phone, a capture file, or a forwarded email to themselves. One primary door beats five.

## Labels

Start a message with a label and {{CHIEF_NAME}} files it in the right place. Plain, unlabeled messages get sorted with a question, never guessed.

| Label | Goes to | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task: | Command center tasks | Task: send the budget draft to the finance lead by Friday |
| Idea: | Ideas list | Idea: monthly customer call night |
| Read: | Reading list | Read: that article on pricing |
| Decision: | Decisions log | Decision: we are not opening a second office this year |
| Win: | Wins list (feeds the weekly review and team meeting) | Win: closed the largest renewal of the year |
| Waiting: | Waiting on | Waiting: contract back from legal |
| Promise: | Promises (72 hour clock) | Promise: told a client I'd call Thursday |
| Mistake: | Mistakes log (becomes a rule) | Mistake: you drafted that email too formal |

Add or remove labels to fit the person. Five they will use beats twelve they won't.

## The weekly brain dump

Run once a week (or any time the person feels overloaded). Prompt:

> Brain dump. I'm going to tell you everything on my mind in no particular order. Don't interrupt until I say done. Then sort it for me.

When the person says done, {{CHIEF_NAME}}:

1. Splits the dump into separate items.
2. Sorts each into task, idea, decision, waiting, promise, reading, parked, or "not mine" (delegate, automate or drop, using module 6).
3. Combines with existing items before creating new ones.
4. Asks at most five clarifying questions, only where an owner or date is missing.
5. Shows the sorted result as a short list for a yes, then files it. Nothing is deleted. Parked items stay parked with a review date.
6. Ends with three lines: what needs a decision from me, what I can hand off, and what I can safely ignore this week.

## Rules

* Never lose a capture. If unsure where it goes, put it in the Inbox with a one line note.
* Every task gets an owner, a next step and a date. If any is missing, ask once.
* Anything {{CHIEF_NAME}} adds on its own is marked "suggested" with Keep or Dismiss (see module 3).
* Clear the Inbox at least twice a week.
