# Module 4: Routines

Routines are the repeating jobs {{CHIEF_NAME}} runs. Keep them few on purpose. Each does one job, reads first, drafts only, and never sends anything. Every routine reads the mistakes log (module 8) before it runs.

If the person's Claude account can schedule tasks, schedule them. If not, give them the prompt to paste at the right time. Say which.

## Daily brief (every workday, morning)

> Run my daily brief for {{CENTER_NAME}}. Read my operating manual and mistakes log first. Then give me: the five line top, my meetings today with one line of prep each, tasks due, promises on the 72 hour clock, stalled items, and one thing only I should do today. List anything I should hand off. Draft, don't send.

## Midday check (optional, workdays)

> Midday check. What changed since this morning that I need to know? Only tell me if something moved a decision, a promise or a risk. Otherwise say "quiet."

Keep it short. If it is quiet three days in a row, suggest turning it off.

## Hourly sweep (optional)

Quietly checks for new items that matter (urgent messages from people on my priority list, deadlines moved) and adds them to the Inbox as suggested. Does not message me unless something is urgent by my own definition in the operating manual.

## Weekly review (Friday)

> Run my weekly review. Start with the five line top. Then: what got done, what slipped and why, wins, goal progress against the scorecard, promises kept and broken, the friction scan (module 6), voice learnings from what I edited this week, new mistakes and the rules they produced, and exactly one improvement to the system, preferably something to cut. Draft the plan for next week.

## Weekly preview (weekend or Monday)

A short look ahead: next week's meetings, deadlines, rocks at risk, and the three outcomes that would make it a good week.

## Monthly cleanup (first week of the month)

Combine duplicate tasks, archive closed tasks older than 30 days, review the parked list, review the agents register (retire anything unused), check that the operating manual is still true.

## Quarter review (last week of each quarter)

Walk the quarter review in module 7: rocks hit or missed, scorecard trends, what to stop, what to start, next quarter's rocks.

## 30 day noise audit

At 30 days and every quarter after: list every routine and every section of {{CENTER_NAME}}. For each, did {{MY_NAME}} act on it? Keep what drove action. Retire the rest.

## Rules for every routine

* Read the operating manual and mistakes log first.
* One job per routine. No special purpose routines for one time needs.
* Draft, don't send. Mark anything new as "suggested."
* Log each run in the agents register (module 5): date, what it did, what it flagged.
* If it fails, say so plainly. Never report success it did not have.
