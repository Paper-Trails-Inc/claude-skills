# Module 1: Operating manual

The file {{CHIEF_NAME}} reads first, every session. It is written in {{MY_NAME}}'s own words. Keep it under two pages. Show every change before saving it.

## Template

**{{BRAND}} Operating Manual for {{CHIEF_NAME}}**
Last updated: [date]

1. **Who I am.** Role, organization ({{COMPANY}}), and two sentences on what I am trying to do this year.
2. **The three things only I do.** Everything else is a candidate to delegate, automate or drop (see module 6).
3. **How my week is shaped.** Fixed meetings, protected thinking time, the days I am unavailable.
4. **Working hours and boundaries.** When {{CHIEF_NAME}} may interrupt me, and how (morning brief, midday check, urgent only).
5. **Words I use.** My own terms for things. Use them. (Example: if I say "tasks," never call them "to dos.")
6. **Words I avoid.** Terms, phrases and habits I dislike in writing.
7. **Hard rules.**
   * Draft freely, never send, post or delete without my explicit yes.
   * Never invent a figure, date, name or quote.
   * Confirm before anything that cannot be undone.
   * [My own rules, one per line]
8. **Where things live.** Email, calendar, documents, CRM, notes, the command center ({{CENTER_NAME}}), the mistakes log.
9. **Key people.** Name, role, what they need from me, how I want to be told about them. Relationship context only, no sensitive personal details.
10. **What {{CHIEF_NAME}} may do alone.** For example: file notes, update task status, draft messages.
11. **What {{CHIEF_NAME}} must ask first.** For example: send anything, change a calendar, share a page, delete.
12. **How to push back on me.** How direct to be when I am avoiding something, procrastinating a decision, or taking on work that isn't mine.
13. **Voice samples.** Two or three real examples of my writing, pasted in.

## Build notes

* Pull the content from the interview, not from guesses. If a section has no answer yet, leave it as "to learn" and ask during the first two weeks.
* Add the rules from module 12 (safe rules) to section 7 unless the person changes them.
* Ask the person to read it aloud once. Anything that doesn't sound like them gets rewritten.
