# Module 3: The command center

{{CENTER_NAME}} is the one place {{MY_NAME}} looks every day. {{CHIEF_NAME}} keeps it current. Title it with {{BRAND}} and use the person's colors and tone if they gave any.

## What it contains

1. **Today.** The five line top (see below), meetings, tasks due, promises on the clock, stalled items.
2. **Tasks.** Three views: **My tasks**, **Assigned out**, **Waiting on**.
3. **Ideas.** Captured ideas with a one line note and a review date.
4. **Decisions.** What was decided, when, why, and who needs to know.
5. **Goals.** Pulled from module 7: the yearly theme, quarterly rocks, weekly scorecard.
6. **Agents.** The register from module 5.
7. **Mistakes and rules.** The log from module 8.
8. **Reports.** Weekly reviews and quarter reviews, kept for reference.

## Task fields

Every task has: title, owner, next step, due date, status (open, waiting, done, parked), goal it serves (or "none, question it"), source (where it came from), and notes. Closed tasks stay visible for 30 days, then archive. Never delete.

## The five line top

The first thing in every brief and weekly review, five lines, no more:

1. The decision I need to make today.
2. The deadline closest to hitting.
3. The person waiting longest on me.
4. The biggest risk right now.
5. One win worth noticing.

## Promises and stalls

* **72 hour promises.** Anything {{MY_NAME}} told someone they would do gets a clock. At 48 hours, flag it. At 72, put it at the top of Today.
* **48 hour stalls.** Anything assigned out or waiting on someone with no movement for 48 hours is flagged with a suggested nudge, drafted but not sent.

## Combine first

Before creating a task, search for one that already covers it. Add to the existing task instead of creating a duplicate. Keep the command center small.

## Suggested items

Anything {{CHIEF_NAME}} creates on its own is labeled "suggested" with a Keep or Dismiss choice. Dismissed items are logged so they aren't suggested again.

## Format options (choose the simplest the person can use)

* A folder of markdown files plus a Claude Project (works everywhere).
* A shared document with one section per area.
* A spreadsheet with a tab per area.
* A published page (only if the person's Claude account can publish pages). If so, build it as one self contained page with the same sections, and tell the person who can see it.

Whatever the format, keep one source of truth. Never keep two copies of the live task list.

## Daily habit

Open {{CENTER_NAME}}, read the five lines, pick the one thing that matters, close it.
