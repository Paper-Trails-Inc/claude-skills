# Module 9: Management team dashboard

Optional. Build this only if {{MY_NAME}} leads a team that meets regularly. It is the shared page for the weekly leadership meeting, kept current by {{CHIEF_NAME}} and shown only to the leaders in the room.

Title it "{{BRAND}} Management Team" (or the name the person prefers).

## Who can see it

Only {{MY_NAME}} and the named leaders. Say plainly who that is before building, and confirm in writing. Department staff who are not in the meeting reply to {{MY_NAME}} by email instead, and {{CHIEF_NAME}} fills their rows from those replies. Never put HR discipline, health, pay, or client sensitive details on this page.

## Tabs

1. **Meeting.** The agenda for the next meeting, in order, with a time for each item. Default 60 minutes: wins (5), scorecard (5), rocks (5), department roundup (10), issues (25), actions and parking lot (5), wrap up (5).
2. **Scorecard.** The weekly numbers from module 7, one row per measure, with owner, target and trend.
3. **Rocks.** The quarterly rocks, owner, status and next step.
4. **Issues.** A list of problems to solve together. Each has an owner, a one line description, and the outcome wanted. Work them in priority order: identify, discuss, decide.
5. **Wins.** What went well this week, by name. Collected all week from capture labels.
6. **Plan.** The yearly theme, goals and calendar.
7. **People.** Birthdays, work anniversaries, holidays and time off coming up.
8. **Department roundup.** One row per department: the update, a number, one thing needing help.
9. **Actions.** What was agreed, who owns it, due date. Carried from meeting to meeting until done.
10. **Parking lot.** Topics that came up but don't belong in this meeting. Reviewed monthly.
11. **Training.** One topic for the team's next learning session, with two or three options for the leader to pick from.
12. **Culture moment (optional).** A short recurring segment the team enjoys: a service standard of the week, a guessing game, a skill share. Ask what the team likes. If nobody joins in, drop it and revisit later.
13. **Chief of staff inbox.** Messages from the other leaders' chiefs of staff (module 11).

## Weekly flow

* **Day before the meeting:** {{CHIEF_NAME}} runs the meeting prep. It updates the scorecard from the latest numbers, pulls wins, fills the department roundup from the replies it has, lists open actions, and drafts the agenda. It marks every missing number "missing" and never estimates.
* **After the meeting:** capture actions, decisions and issues the same day, assign owners, and set due dates.
* **If {{MY_NAME}} is out:** {{CHIEF_NAME}} flags that the meeting may need to be cancelled or run by someone else. {{MY_NAME}} decides. Never automatic.

## Weekly update requests (optional)

Draft a short email each week asking each department lead for: one win, one number, one thing that needs help. Replies feed the roundup. Drafted by {{CHIEF_NAME}}, sent by {{MY_NAME}}.

## Rules

* Never invent a number. "Missing" is a valid answer.
* The page is a tool for the meeting, not a report card on individuals. Keep tone factual and kind.
* Retire any tab nobody uses after 30 days.
* If the page is published, confirm who can view it every time it changes.
